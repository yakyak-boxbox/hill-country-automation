Call scripts
Every line you need — from opening to close.
Opening — set the frame
"Thanks for jumping on. Here's how I want to use our time — I'm going to ask you a few questions about how your business operates, and by the end I'll show you specifically where AI could save you the most time. Sound good?"
You're running the call. This framing prevents them from asking about price too early.
The golden question
"What's the most repetitive task you do every single week that you wish you could just make disappear?"
The most important question on the call. Let them talk. Don't interrupt. The longer they go the more they feel the pain.
After the demo — the close
"Based on what you told me I'd build you [specific automation] — delivered in 48 hours for $400. Half now, half on delivery. Full refund if it doesn't save you 5 hours a week. Want to move forward?"
State the price once and stop talking. First person to speak loses. Let the silence sit.
Objection — "I need to think about it"
"Totally fair. What's the main thing you'd be thinking through? I'd rather just address it now so you're not sitting on it."
Objection — "It's too expensive"
"I hear that. You said this takes you about [X hours] a week — at your time's value that's costing you more than $400 a month to keep doing manually. This pays for itself in the first month."
The agent upsell — after delivery
"You've been using it for a week now. I can also set this up so it runs completely automatically — every new lead gets a reply within 2 minutes without you doing anything. That's $450 a month and I handle everything. Want me to set that up?"
This is the jump from a one-time build to a monthly agent. Same technology — completely different value proposition.
